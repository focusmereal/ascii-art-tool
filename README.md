## 🦅 A powerful ASCII art generator with 15+ fonts, 12+ colors, and animations

### ✨ Features

- 🎨 **15+ Fonts**: standard, slant, bubble, block, 3D, small, big, doom, graffiti, mini, star, fire, matrix, ghost, neon
- 🌈 **12+ Colors**: rainbow, fire, ice, matrix, neon, pastel, random, and standard colors
- 🎬 **Animation**: Watch your art come to life with line-by-line animation
- 💾 **Save to File**: Export your creations to text files
- 📜 **History**: Keep track of your last 100 creations
- 🎲 **Random Mode**: Generate random ASCII art with one command
- 🖥️ **CLI & Interactive**: Both command-line and interactive modes
